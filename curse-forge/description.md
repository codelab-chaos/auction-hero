# Auction Hero

## One of the easiest Auction House addons you have ever used!!!

**An early access work in progress!!**

Auction Hero replaces the stock auction house window on **World of Warcraft: Forever** with one built for buying, selling and reselling. Everything it shows comes from your own scans. There is no outside price feed and nothing to set up.

### What it does

- **Buy:** a fresh filter layout built around finding better gear. Pick a slot from the rail, then toggle **Better** for anything that beats what you wear (stats weighted for your class) or **Picks** for upgrades you can wear today, sorted by how much they gain. Quality, category, near-level and search filters are one click away, and a deal meter shows how each price compares with what the item usually goes for.
- **Sell:** your bags as a grid, price rules (match, undercut, quick sale and more), how confident the price is, and the competition you would be listed against.
- **Resell:** small markets you could buy out and relist at a profit.
- **Auctions:** what you have up for sale, with cancelling in two presses, like the game's own window.
- **Ledger:** every auction house trade your character makes and what each item has made you.
- **Graphs:** price and supply for an item over 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.
- **Item tooltips:** hover any item in the game for its going rate, lowest price, quantity for sale and when it was last seen.
- **Recipe tooltips:** hover a profession recipe for the crafted item's price, the material cost and the margin.
- **Progress and Hero Coins:** scan confidence, a daily scan streak, and Hero Coins earned just by using the addon. Can you spend them? ...TBD

### Getting started

1. Install, then open the auction house. Auction Hero opens with it (or type `/ah`).
2. Press **Scan**. The first full scan takes a moment; the server allows one every 15 minutes.
3. Shop, sell and flip. The more days you scan, the surer the prices get.

`/ah help` lists the commands.

### Early access

Auction Hero is young and changing fast.

- Scanning, price history, posting, cancelling and buying goods sold by the unit work.
- Buying single items (gear) and buying straight from the Resell tab are still being built.
- The server will not take a single item posted for less than 1 silver (it hangs the auction house instead of refusing). Auction Hero will not send one.

Found a bug or have an idea? Please open an issue. Screenshots help a lot.
